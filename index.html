About The Founders

Bay and her sister Magnolia have been enjoying 3-D printed toys since they received their first, small 3-D printer in 2024. After upgrading to a larger printer in 2025, they decided they wanted to share the joy of 3-D printed toys with others. Their main goal is to deliver smiles and fun right to your front door.
